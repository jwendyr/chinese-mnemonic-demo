Ciri Khas Ortografi & Linguistik

  • Alfabet pertama dalam sejarah yang memisahkan huruf vokal penuh (Α, Ε, Η, Ι, Ο, Υ, Ω) dan konsonan secara sejajar.
  • Huruf Sigma memiliki dua bentuk huruf kecil: σ di awal/tengah kata dan ς di akhir kata.
  • Menjadi sumber langsung bagi simbol fisika, matematika, dan kimia (π, Δ, Ω, λ, μ, θ).

Greek was first written in Mycenae with a script known as Linear B, which was used between about 1500 and 1200 BC. This variety of Greek is known as Mycenaean. On Crete another script, known as the Cypriot syllabary, was used to write the local variety of Greek between about 1200 and 300 BC.
