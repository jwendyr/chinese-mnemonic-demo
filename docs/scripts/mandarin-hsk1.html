Ringkasan Ensiklopedia (Sumber Omniglot)

During the reign of the Jin (1115-1234) and Yuan (1271-1368) dynasties, a common language, known as Old Mandarin began to emerge in northern China. It was used in literature, along Classical/Literary Chinese, and written with either the Chinese script, or the 'Phags-pa script. Mandarin, in some form or another, has been used as the lingua franca in China since the 14th century, particularly among officials. Until the middle of the 19th century this lingua franca was based on Nanjing dialects, but since then northern dialects, particularly the Beijing dialect, have risen to prominence.
