**ID.** Daftar belajar berisi bentuk tradisional, bacaan, dan arti ringkas Indonesia serta Inggris. Jalur Taiwan mencantumkan Zhuyin dan Pinyin. Jalur Kanton mencantumkan Jyutping dengan angka nada. ID entri dapat dipakai untuk mengaitkan kartu mnemonik dan bacaan. Contohnya, **菜** di Taiwan dibaca **ㄘㄞˋ / cài** dan berarti *sayur, hidangan*. Di Kanton **食飯** dibaca **sik6 faan6** dan berarti *makan*.

**EN.** The study lists include traditional forms, readings, and short Indonesian and English glosses. The Taiwan track includes Zhuyin and Pinyin. The Cantonese track uses tone-numbered Jyutping. Use entry IDs to link mnemonic cards and readings. For example, Taiwan Mandarin **菜** is **ㄘㄞˋ / cài** and means *vegetables, dish*. Cantonese **食飯** is **sik6 faan6** and means *to have a meal*.

## Mandarin Taiwan · TBCL

**ID.** Salinan workbook NAER memberi **184 kosakata pada 1** dan **216 pada 1\***. Aksara terbagi menjadi **163 pada 1** dan **83 pada 1\***. Tanda bintang menandai bagian level yang relatif lebih sulit. Halaman deskripsi TBCL mencatat 396 kosakata. Katalog mengambil semua 400 baris dari workbook kosakata April 2025 dan mempertahankan urutan, varian ejaan, dan ID sumber. Akhiran penanda makna seperti angka pada **得2** tersimpan di kolom `source_word`. [Deskripsi TBCL](https://coct.naer.edu.tw/page.jsp?ID=11) · [Panduan NAER](https://coct.naer.edu.tw/file/files/%E8%8F%AF%E8%AA%9E%E6%96%87%E8%83%BD%E5%8A%9B%E5%9F%BA%E6%BA%96%E6%87%89%E7%94%A8%E5%8F%83%E8%80%83%E6%8C%87%E5%BC%95.pdf)

**EN.** The downloaded workbooks contain **184 vocabulary rows at 1** and **216 at 1\***, plus **163 character rows at 1** and **83 at 1\***. The asterisk marks material of relatively higher difficulty within a level. The TBCL description page reports 396 words. This catalog includes all 400 rows from the April 2025 vocabulary workbook, preserving their source order, spelling variants, and IDs. Sense labels such as the digit in **得2** remain in `source_word`. [TBCL descriptor](https://coct.naer.edu.tw/page.jsp?ID=11) · [NAER guide](https://coct.naer.edu.tw/file/files/%E8%8F%AF%E8%AA%9E%E6%96%87%E8%83%BD%E5%8A%9B%E5%9F%BA%E6%BA%96%E6%87%89%E7%94%A8%E5%8F%83%E8%80%83%E6%8C%87%E5%BC%95.pdf)

**ID.** Data dasar berasal dari National Academy for Educational Research (NAER), berlisensi **CC BY** sesuai laman unduhan. Arti ID/EN dan bacaan aksara tunggal ditambahkan untuk proyek. Pada **這裡 / 這裏 / 這兒**, bacaan disejajarkan dengan tiga bentuk yang tertulis. Pada **有時**, Zhuyin diselaraskan ke **ㄧㄡˇ ㄕˊ**. [Unduhan dan atribusi NAER](https://coct.naer.edu.tw/page.jsp?ID=41) · [Workbook kosakata](sources/naer-vocabulary.xlsx) · [Workbook aksara](sources/naer-characters.xlsx)

**EN.** The underlying data comes from Taiwan’s National Academy for Educational Research (NAER), under the **CC BY** terms stated on its download page. ID/EN glosses and character readings are project additions. The entry **這裡 / 這裏 / 這兒** has its readings aligned to the three written variants. The Zhuyin for **有時** is normalized to **ㄧㄡˇ ㄕˊ**. [NAER downloads and attribution](https://coct.naer.edu.tw/page.jsp?ID=41) · [Vocabulary workbook](sources/naer-vocabulary.xlsx) · [Character workbook](sources/naer-characters.xlsx)

## Kanton Hong Kong / Hong Kong Cantonese

**ID.** Urutan pemula mengikuti delapan bab **Cantonese Self-learning Class** dari The Education University of Hong Kong (EdUHK). Bab berfungsi sebagai label kurikulum. Ke-184 entri memuat 178 bentuk tulisan yang berbeda. Pengulangan lintas bab dipertahankan untuk konteks dan makna yang diajarkan di masing-masing bab. Arti ID/EN ditulis untuk katalog. Kolom `source_pos` menyimpan label kursus apa adanya. [Kursus EdUHK](https://cantonese-self-learning-class.eduhk.hk/) · [Arsip data bab](sources/eduhk-vocabulary.json)

**EN.** The starter sequence follows the eight chapters of the **Cantonese Self-learning Class** from The Education University of Hong Kong (EdUHK). Chapter numbers are curriculum labels. The 184 lesson entries contain 178 distinct written forms. Repeated entries retain their lesson-specific senses and contexts. The ID/EN glosses were written for this catalog. The `source_pos` column retains the course labels as published. [EdUHK course](https://cantonese-self-learning-class.eduhk.hk/) · [Chapter data snapshot](sources/eduhk-vocabulary.json)

**ID.** Pada entri **考**, katalog memakai **haau2** sesuai kamus CUHK. **白車** dibaca **baak6 ce1**. Kursus juga mencantumkan bacaan untuk padanan **救護車**, yaitu **gau3 wu6 ce1**. Varian dan bacaan yang disunting dapat dilacak lewat kolom sumber. [CUHK · 考](https://humanum.arts.cuhk.edu.hk/Lexis/lexi-can/pho-rel.php?s1=h&s2=aau&s3=2)

**EN.** The catalog uses **haau2** for **考**, as listed by CUHK. **白車** reads **baak6 ce1**. The course also includes **gau3 wu6 ce1**, the reading of its equivalent **救護車**. See the source columns for edited spellings and readings. [CUHK · 考](https://humanum.arts.cuhk.edu.hk/Lexis/lexi-can/pho-rel.php?s1=h&s2=aau&s3=2)
